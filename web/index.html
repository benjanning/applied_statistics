05 / METHODS AND LIMITS

Evidence, with its assumptions.

Welch methods allow unequal variances, but still rely on independent observations and approximate distributional assumptions. Ten measurements per condition give limited information about those assumptions. The plots make spread and unusual observations visible; no observation is removed and no diagnostic p-value is used to choose a more favourable test.

Mean intervals are pointwise 95% t intervals. Difference intervals and p-values use a three-comparison Bonferroni adjustment; they are different quantities. Overlapping mean intervals are not a pairwise significance test. A p-value is not the probability that a hypothesis is true, an effect size or proof of general effectiveness.

These are descriptive and model-based comparisons of the preserved R PlantGrowth teaching data. The CSV cannot verify the original experimental design or independence. There is no replication study, preregistered analysis or general treatment recommendation.
